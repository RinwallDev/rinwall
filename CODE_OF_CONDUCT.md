# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1. The full text is at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/ and applies here in its
entirety; what follows is the part people actually need day to day.

## The standard

Be direct about the work and decent to the people. Disagree with code, designs, and
findings as hard as the evidence supports - this is a security project and soft reviews
help nobody. Do not make it about the person: no harassment, no insults, no publishing
anyone's private information, no sexualized language or attention, no sustained
disruption of discussions.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any channel representing the
project, and to anyone participating - maintainers included.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to the maintainers through GitHub's private vulnerability
reporting on this repository (it opens a private thread even though the subject isn't a
vulnerability), or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled privately,
and the people involved in a report are never the ones who decide its outcome.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove or edit comments, commits, and other contributions that violate
this standard, and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban anyone whose
behavior they judge inappropriate, threatening, or harmful. The Covenant's enforcement
guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban) are the ladder used.
